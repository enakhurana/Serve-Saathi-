Final MVP product rules applied (approved — not re-flagged)

  1. SOS/Emergency — visible in every header, never gated by plan.
  2. Multi-elder — one plan per family account; 2nd+ elder discount only, no mixed tiers.
  3. Upgrade — immediate, full new-plan price charged at checkout, no proration shown.
  4. Downgrade — effective at end of current billing period; benefits retained until then.
  5. Cancellation — effective at end of current billing period; no refund or partial credit.
  6. Payment failure — neutral "limited period" copy; no invented grace-period length.
  7. Renewal reminder — generic "renews soon" + real date; no hard-coded day count.
  8. GST — "+ applicable taxes" shown throughout; Plan price / Taxes / Total itemized at checkout; no invented %.
  9. Skip for now — on plan browsing, new-signup plan choice, and non-essential prompts; never on payment, confirmation, cancellation, or SOS.
